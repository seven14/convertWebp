WebP → PNG

Drop in .webp images and get lossless PNG copies with transparency intact. Animated WebPs can become an APNG, a PNG frame sequence, or an animated AVIF. Files never leave your device.

Files

Saving isn't available in this view. Right-click (or long-press) a thumbnail and choose “Save image”, or open this page in claude.ai.

PNG is lossless, so output files are often larger than the WebP source.

Browsers can't encode animated AVIF yet, so the AVIF option gives you a ZIP with every frame plus a one-click script that builds a lossless .avif with the free avifenc tool.
